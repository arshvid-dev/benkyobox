Sorry dude, "okimasu" is in the wrong format for the conjugator.

You must enter the dictionary form in Romaji - this always ends in "u" - e.g. taberu, miru, iku etc (but NOT normally "-masu" - that's the present polite form!). Alternatively you can enter the dictionary form in Japanese script if it has been entered on our list of verbs Please try again or choose one of the options further down the page
